Interpreter and Teaching Assistant

Southern Utah University · Internship

Bilingual classroom support and Excel course records for an SUU English writing program serving 200+ students in Wuhan.

May 2026 · Wuhan, China

Role & Context

I served as interpreter and teaching assistant for Professor Sharon Lyman's Southern Utah University English writing program at Wuhan Polytechnic University in May 2026. The program served 200+ students. I supported classroom communication and organized course records and scores.

Classroom Support

I provided bilingual support and interpreted between the professor and the students. I helped students understand the professor, and I helped the professor understand how teaching and communication work in a Chinese classroom. Since students' English ranged widely, I adjusted my explanations and feedback to each level.

Course Operations

I organized course records and scores for 200+ students in Excel. I managed attendance, graded assignments against the professor's rubric, gave written feedback, and prepared final-grade spreadsheets and course-completion reports.

Cultural Bridge

Working between English and Chinese taught me to slow down and check what each person had understood. When a literal translation missed the context, I asked another question before explaining. The role depended as much on listening as translating.
