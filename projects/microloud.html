Microloud

Developing an AI-powered marketing tool to help artists with limited budgets attract potential clients.

Artist marketing tool · Jul 2026 - Present

In development

The problem

Artists with limited marketing budgets need a practical way to reach potential clients. Microloud explores how AI can support that work while keeping the artist's voice and goals at the center.

Work to date

I defined the target audiences and channel roles, developed brand positioning, and created a weekly content plan. This work provides a direction for the tool's development.

The project is still in development. The material presented here is the current strategy and measurement plan.

Measurement plan

The plan tracks content engagement and visits from campaign links to understand which messages and channels bring interest. As the tool develops, actual inquiries and client acquisition will need to be measured separately.

The next step is to test the product's usefulness and use the feedback to refine it.
